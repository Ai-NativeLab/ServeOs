- [x] **Step 4: Compute at issuance.** In `issueRefund`, after the line amounts are settled and inside the existing tx: derive the order's VAT-per-line using `allocateLargestRemainder` from `@/server/fiscal/decimal` (pure module — one-line comment sanctioning the cross-domain import of a side-effect-free util) with weights = each order item's VAT-bearing net (mirror `computeOrderTotals`' basis: deliveryFee excluded, serviceCharge in-base — read `src/lib/order-totals.ts:57-70` and cite it); a refunded line's `vatAmount` = its allocated order-line VAT × refundedQty/orderQty at 2dp via the same decimal helpers; headerless full = remaining VAT (order VAT − Σ prior refund `vatAmount`), goodwill-scaled by `totalAmount/outstanding`. INVARIANT (throw, matching house style): Σ all refunds' `vatAmount` for an order may never exceed `orders.vatAmount`. **As built (two documented deviations, both in `computeRefundVat`'s JSDoc):** (1) the service charge gets its OWN weight bucket — `orders.vatAmount` is split across `[orders.subtotal, orders.serviceChargeAmount]` first, then the item bucket across `order_items.lineTotal` — so a line's stored VAT is exactly the VAT inside the money handed back for it; folding the service charge into item weights would over-reverse (6.76 stored against 6.14 actually contained). Consequence: an itemised refund never returns the service charge's VAT share; only a full/headerless refund does. (2) each item's VAT is split largest-remainder across its UNITS and a refund takes the units not yet returned, instead of `× refundedQty/orderQty` half-up — the latter reverses 8.20 of an 8.19 VAT line over two 1-of-2 refunds, tripping the invariant on a legitimate refund. Invariant throws `PosRefundError` (400, house style) naming prior/this/order VAT AND the remedy ("issue this as a full refund without item lines instead"); it is reachable only across paths (goodwill full refund, then itemising the same units) and fails closed rather than clamping. **Spec-review follow-up (same task, second commit):** an itemised `full` refund whose payments clear the outstanding balance now TOPS UP its line VATs to the remaining order VAT (residual spread largest-remainder over the line amounts, quantities as fallback when a full refund's lines carry no amounts), so `Σ lines == header == remaining` and the service charge's share is never orphaned — worked figures pinned by test: single 100.00 item under a 10% service charge stores `13.51` (12.28 + a 1.23 top-up); after a prior 33.33 partial on a two-line order it tops up to `13.92` (12.28 + 1.64), not to the full 18.01. Partial refunds are byte-identical (the original 9 tests unchanged); a full refund of LESS than the balance is goodwill and gets no top-up. **Spec re-review follow-up (third commit):** the residual can be NEGATIVE (a prior goodwill refund already took VAT unattributed), and a pro-rata scale-down could drive an individual line below zero — an invented input tax credit on the return document — because a full refund's line amounts are never validated against its tenders and can point the opposite way to the VAT the items carry. `floorLineVatsAt0` now clips offenders to `0.00` and re-spreads the shortfall over the lines still holding VAT (excluding zeroed lines from the allocation, since a zero weight sorts FIRST for a negative total and would be handed a leftover cent), so the ceiling is enforced PER LINE with `Σ lines == header` intact. Pinned: 190.00/10.00 items (23.33/1.23) after a 24.00 goodwill refund, refunded full with inverted 10.00/190.00 line amounts → raw 22.13/−21.57 floors to **0.56 / 0.00** against a 0.56 header; plain negative residual (goodwill 12.28, then itemised full of both units) → `12.28`; zero-amount full-refund lines fall back to quantity weights instead of throwing "every weight is zero".
