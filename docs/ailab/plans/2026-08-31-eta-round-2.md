- [ ] **POS partial refunds offer the NET `lineTotal`, so a VAT-exclusive tenant's return under-reverses VAT by the difference** (surfaced by Task 2; recorded for its own fix): `apps/pos/src/screens/SalesHistory.tsx` builds a refund line as `amount: Number(r.item.lineTotal)` and the partial-refund rule forces the tender to equal it, so refunding one 100.00 item under ServeOS's default VAT-exclusive pricing pays back 100.00 — which contains 12.28 of VAT, not the 14.00 the refund domain attributes to that line. The return receipt reports what the money contains (12.28), because reporting 14.00 would claim credit for tax the customer never got back and ETA would reject it outright under Main Calculations rule 42. Under-reversing is the safe side, so this is a correctness improvement rather than a live exposure. **The observable, and what makes it worth a ticket rather than a footnote:** until it lands, a VAT-exclusive tenant's ETA-reported output VAT will NOT tie back to their own refund ledger — `Σ eta_submissions` return-receipt `taxTotals` comes in below `Σ refunds.vatAmount` by exactly this difference. That reconciliation break is what a tax adviser finds first, and it looks like a filing error rather than the deliberate, safe-direction choice it is. Fix: offer the GROSS amount on a partial refund (POS layer, plus the dashboard refund surface), after which stored and reported VAT agree to the cent in every shape.
